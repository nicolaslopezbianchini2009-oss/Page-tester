Pending legal review

This text covers what the site actually does today. Before publishing, fill in the legal entity and address, and have it reviewed by a lawyer for the jurisdiction you operate in.

Who handles your data

Margen is a service operated by Nicolás López. Any question about your data is answered through the same channel you used to get in touch.

What data I receive

  • 01What you enter in the form: brand or group, number of locations, platforms you run on, order volume, your name and your email.
  • 02If we move to an audit, the operational data in your delivery platform accounts: orders, payouts, claims and location metrics.
  • 03This site uses no tracking cookies and no third-party analytics. The calculator runs entirely in your browser and sends nothing.

What I use it for

Only to reply to you, prepare the audit and, if you become a client, run the service: detect and file claims, generate your reports and keep the monitoring going. I do not sell, rent or share your data with third parties, and I do not use it to train models or build contact lists.

Access to your accounts

When we work together I operate with my own users, created inside your dashboards with the minimum permission required, never with your personal credentials. Every action is logged under those users and you can audit it from your own dashboard. When the engagement ends, you revoke them and the access is cut.

How long I keep it

Contact details from an inquiry that does not move forward are deleted after twelve months. A client's operational data is kept for the length of the engagement and up to twenty-four months afterwards, because claims can be reopened. If you ask for deletion sooner, it is deleted, except for what must be kept for tax or accounting obligations.

Your rights

At any time you can request access to the data I hold about you, its correction, its export or its deletion. You can also withdraw the consent you gave in the form. Write to me and I resolve it within ten business days.

Changes

If anything in this policy changes, the date at the top is updated. If the change affects how I handle data you already gave me, I let you know by email first.
