01 / The problem

The money that leaks out while nobody is looking

It never goes all at once. It goes twenty dollars at a time, every day, across four separate dashboards that nobody opens together. By the time someone sits down to look, the dispute window has already closed.

  • 01Orders the platform cancelled after you already cooked them, and nobody disputed.
  • 02Missing items and refunds charged back to the store with no evidence behind them.
  • 03Platform adjustments and fees that show up on the payout with no explanation.
  • 04Dispute windows that expire in days and get lost because nobody owns them.
  • 05A report built by hand on Monday, with data from one app, already out of date.

08 / Questions

What everyone asks

01Do you get access to my accounts? How do you secure it?

Yes, I need access to operate. I work with my own users, granted the minimum permission required, never with your personal credentials, and they are registered under my name so you can see in the dashboard what each one did. We sign an NDA before anything gets connected, and the day we stop working together you revoke those users and the access ends.

02What happens if a platform changes its system?

It happens often, and it is part of the job. Keeping the automations current when an app changes its dashboard, its payout format or its dispute process is included in the fee: it is not an add-on and it is not an excuse to pause the operation. While the fix is in progress, claims get filed manually so no window is missed.

03Do you still charge if you recover nothing?

The monthly fee is charged either way, because it covers the full operation: reports, alerts and monitoring keep running even in a month with nothing to claim. The percentage, on the other hand, is only charged on money that actually landed back in your account. If nothing is recovered, there is no percentage.

04Am I locked into a long contract?

No. The agreement runs month to month, with thirty days' notice to end it. The only thing I ask is that the first cycle runs in full, because filed claims take time to resolve and that is when the result shows up. The consolidated data generated along the way is yours, exported.

05How long does it take to get started?

The audit takes five business days from the moment I have access, and the proposal arrives forty-eight hours later. If we move forward, implementation takes two weeks and the operation starts on the first day of the following month. In total, three to four weeks from the first call to the first claim filed by the system.
